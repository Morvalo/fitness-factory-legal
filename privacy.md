# Privacy Policy – Fitness Factory

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

Fitness Factory is a personal automation tool operated by Morvalo. It publishes fitness education videos to the operator's own TikTok account only.

## Data we access
Through the TikTok API we access only the operator's own account: basic profile information and the ability to upload and publish videos.

## Data we store
OAuth tokens are stored privately by the operator to keep the integration working. No data about other TikTok users is collected, stored, or shared.

## Third parties
We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
